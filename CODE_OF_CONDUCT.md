# Code of Conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully and professionally.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, publishing another person's private information, deliberate disruption, and abusive or sexually explicit conduct.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
